FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything people ask about getting their AI agents to work together.

What is HiveMind?

HiveMind lets your AI agents collaborate by sharing what they each learn. When one agent learns something, every other agent on every one of your devices can use it too. It runs entirely on hardware you own, with no central server.

How do AI agents share memory in HiveMind?

Your agents do it on their own. As each one works, what it learns lands in a local memory, and your devices stay in step automatically, trading only what is missing, so every agent ends up with the same knowledge without a central broker.

Does HiveMind send my data to the cloud?

No. HiveMind has no cloud, no third-party servers, and no subscriptions. Your memory lives on your own devices and stays current across them over your private network.

Which agents and platforms does HiveMind work with?

HiveMind works with Claude Code, Hermes, and the MCP server for Claude Desktop. It currently runs on Windows 11; Mac and Android support are in progress.

How does HiveMind decide what is trustworthy?

Confidence is earned, not declared. A fact only gains trust when multiple independent agents arrive at it on their own. A single agent cannot inflate its own credibility, and conflicting facts are surfaced rather than silently overwritten.
