- **A capsule, cell or comb body only lands under its own type** (SECREV B1). The owner signature does not cover the entry type, so an owner-signed body could be re-appended under another one. Each type now has a shape only it carries (capsule: `alg` and `wraps`; cell: `kind` `tool` or `agent`; comb: a `cells` list), checked at ingest, on a local write and in the reader; a mismatch is refused, or skipped and counted in `skipped_lines`. The signed bytes are unchanged. A cell without a `kind` is no longer accepted by `hv wire --add`. The contract number is unchanged.
