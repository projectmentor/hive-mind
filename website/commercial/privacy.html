Privacy Policy

Effective June 5, 2026.

HiveMind is built to keep your data on your own machines. This policy covers the small amount of information we collect through this website.

The short version

  • HiveMind software runs locally on hardware you own. Your agents' memory never reaches us.
  • The only personal data we collect is the email address you voluntarily submit for early access.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it except with the service providers that help us operate (for example, email delivery).

Information we collect

Early-access email. If you join the early-access list, we collect the email address you submit and the date you submitted it, so we can contact you about HiveMind.

Basic site analytics. Like most websites, our host may log standard request data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and aggregate traffic measurement.

What we do not collect. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to the contents of your HiveMind corpus, your agents' activity, or any data stored by the HiveMind software on your machines.

How we use it

We use your email only to send you HiveMind updates and early-access information. We will not use it for unrelated marketing, and we will not sell or rent it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email from the list at any time by contacting us at the address below. Every email we send will include an unsubscribe option.

Data retention

We keep your early-access email until you ask us to remove it or until HiveMind no longer maintains an early-access program.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@projectmentor.org.

This policy is provided as a starting template and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.
