Feature

Knowledge that compounds across your agents

Most agent work is thrown away at the end of a session. HiveMind keeps it, so each discovery becomes a foundation rather than a footnote.

Every session builds on the last

When learning persists and is shared, progress accumulates. The fact one agent established today is the starting point for another agent tomorrow.

Over weeks, that compounding is the difference between agents that tread water and agents that get measurably more capable at your work.

The whole team gets smarter

Because the memory is shared, an improvement anywhere benefits everywhere. You are not training one agent; you are growing a collective intelligence.

Frequently asked

Does the memory grow without bound?

HiveMind keeps a full history but filters what is surfaced, so what matters compounds without drowning agents in noise.

Is old knowledge ever wrong?

Facts carry their confidence and where they came from, and conflicts are surfaced, so stale or contradicted beliefs are visible rather than trusted blindly.
