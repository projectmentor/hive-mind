How it works

1
An agent learns something

Any agent — Hermes, Claude Code, or your own — makes a discovery and stores it in HiveMind.

2
HiveMind remembers it

The fact is stored with full context. Who found it, when, and how confident they are. Automatically, with no input from you.

3
Every agent knows immediately

All your agents on all your machines sync and see the new fact. They build on each other's work without you saying a word.
