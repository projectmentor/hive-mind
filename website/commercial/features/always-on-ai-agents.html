Feature

Agents that work while you sleep

The promise of agents is that the work continues when you step away. Collaboration is what makes that real.

Around-the-clock collaboration

Agents on different devices keep reading and writing the same memory whether you are watching or not. Work done overnight is waiting for you, already shared.

You hand off the task and come back to progress, not to a pile of loose ends to reassemble.

Hand off, then step back

Because the memory holds the thread, you do not have to be the one carrying state between agents. They keep each other current.

Frequently asked

Do agents need me online to share what they learn?

No. Nodes sync directly with each other; your presence is not required for memory to propagate.

What do I come back to?

Results and a shared record of what happened, including where each part came from, rather than a set of disconnected session logs.

Take yourself out of the loop.

Let your agents collaborate, and step back.
