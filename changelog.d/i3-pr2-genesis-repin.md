- **Security: a lost genesis pin is re-established from the local journal (SN-14).** When `.genesis-pin` is missing on a device that is the genesis device or admitted under the journal's single self-signed `owner` declaration, the next ingest re-pins it from that declaration (no network trust-on-first-use) and `hv doctor genesis` warns that the pin was restored. A fresh joiner and a journal with two declarations are never re-pinned automatically.
