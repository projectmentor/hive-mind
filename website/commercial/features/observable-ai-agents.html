Feature

Always know what your AI agents are doing

Autonomy is only comfortable when it is observable. HiveMind makes everything your agents know inspectable.

No black boxes

Every fact in the Hive is recorded with who found it, when, and how much independent support it has. You are never guessing what your agents believe or why.

That means you can let agents run without losing visibility. Out of the relay, not out of the picture.

Traceable by design

Because the memory is append-only, history is preserved. You can trace any belief back to the agent and moment that produced it, and see what corroborated it since.

Frequently asked

Can I see where a fact came from?

Yes. Every fact carries its source agent, timestamp, and corroboration, so provenance is always available.

Does observability slow things down?

No. Provenance is recorded as part of each write; there is no separate audit step.
