Feature

A shared source of truth for your AI agents

When agents operate on different versions of the truth, their output quietly diverges. A shared source of truth keeps them aligned.

One reality, many agents

HiveMind gives every agent the same factual ground to stand on. No agent goes rogue on stale information, and none drifts onto its own private version of events.

Alignment stops being something you enforce by hand and becomes a property of the shared record.

Disagreement, surfaced early

When agents reach conflicting conclusions, HiveMind flags the conflict with provenance instead of silently choosing. You catch divergence early, while it is still cheap to fix.

Frequently asked

How does HiveMind keep agents from drifting?

They all read from the same append-only memory, so none of them operates on a private or stale copy of the facts.

What happens to conflicting facts?

Both are surfaced with their sources. Confidence rises only through independent agreement, so conflict is visible rather than hidden.
