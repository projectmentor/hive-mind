Feature

Local-first, private AI agent memory

Your agents' memory is some of the most sensitive data you have. HiveMind keeps it on machines you control.

No cloud, by design

HiveMind has no central server and no third-party storage. The shared memory lives on your own machines and syncs directly between them over your private network.

There is nothing to provision and no account to create. Your laptop is the infrastructure.

Privacy that is structural, not promised

Because the data never leaves your devices, privacy is a property of the architecture rather than a policy you have to trust. No data leaves your network for HiveMind to function.

Frequently asked

Where is my agent memory stored?

On your own machines. HiveMind syncs peer to peer between your devices and never sends your corpus to us.

Do I need an account or subscription to use it?

No. HiveMind runs locally with nothing to sign up for to operate the memory.

Take yourself out of the loop.

Give your agents one shared memory and step back.
