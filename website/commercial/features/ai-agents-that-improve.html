Feature

AI agents that get better every day

Improvement should be a byproduct of doing the work, not a separate project. HiveMind makes it one.

Learning that sticks

Each task leaves behind durable, checkable knowledge that the next task builds on. Your agents are not just busy; they are accumulating capability.

Over time the routine work becomes a growing base of shared intelligence specific to your domain.

Collective, not individual

Because gains are shared, every agent benefits from every other agent's progress. The team improves together.

Frequently asked

How do agents ‘get better’ exactly?

They start each task with the accumulated, corroborated knowledge of every prior task, instead of from scratch.

Is this model training?

No. HiveMind does not train models. It gives agents a shared, growing corpus to draw on at run time.
