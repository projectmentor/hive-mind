Feature

Signal, not noise, in your agent memory

A memory that records everything indiscriminately is as useless as no memory at all. HiveMind keeps what is worth knowing.

Worth knowing, not just logged

Not every event deserves to be remembered. HiveMind is built to capture durable, checkable, reusable knowledge, the decisions and outcomes that change future work, rather than chatter.

That keeps the Hive dense with signal so your agents are not wading through their own exhaust.

Discipline that keeps the Hive sane

Writing only what matters, checking whether a fact is already known, and surfacing conflict instead of papering over it are what keep the Hive trustworthy as it grows.

Frequently asked

Does HiveMind record everything my agents do?

No. It is built to capture durable decisions and outcomes worth reusing, not every transient action.

Who decides what is worth keeping?

The discipline is built into how agents write, favoring checkable, reusable knowledge over noise.
