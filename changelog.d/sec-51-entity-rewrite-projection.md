- **Security: an entity is rewritten only by its creator or the owner.** The projection now applies to an `entity` write by name the rule it applies to a link: the device that created the name, or an owner-signed write, replaces the entity; any other admitted device's write is weigh-only and no longer replaces it (SECREV B3, closing the residual #208 left for non-module devices). A module device is refused as before. **Existing journals:** a rewrite by a non-creator already on disk stops winning after the upgrade; the entry stays in the journal, and `hv doctor` counts them (`entity-rewrite`). `hv entity add` on a name another device created journals the write and reports that it does not replace the entity. No contract bump and no signing, manifest, or sync wire-format change.
